Flashcards and Active Recall: A Simple Way to Study Better

Flashcards are cards with a question or word on one side and the answer on the other. When you use them, you try to remember the answer before flipping the card. This is called active recall, which means using your memory instead of just rereading notes.

Active recall helps you remember information better and for a longer time. It also shows you what you still need to study. Using flashcards this way is a simple and effective way to learn.
